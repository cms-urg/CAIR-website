Steve Joordens is a Professor of Psychology at the University of Toronto Scarborough, where he has been a faculty member since 1995. He received his PhD in Psychology from the University of Waterloo and completed postdoctoral work at McMaster University on models and empirical studies of human memory.

His research spans cognitive psychology, especially human memory and the interplay of conscious and unconscious influences, and educational psychology, including peer assessment, critical thinking, and the effective use of technology for learning. He co-developed peerScholar, a web-based platform for peer assessment that received a National Technology Innovation Award, and has taught introductory psychology to tens of thousands of learners online.

His teaching has been recognised with the University of Toronto President's Teaching Award and the OCUFA Teaching Award, and he was named one of Ontario's top post-secondary lecturers in TVO's Best Lecturer competition. In the lab, he brings cognitive science to the design of conversational agents and to AI-supported assessment and feedback.
