Brian Harrington is a Professor, Teaching Stream, in the Department of Computer and Mathematical Sciences at the University of Toronto Scarborough, and a Faculty Affiliate of the Schwartz Reisman Institute for Technology and Society.

He completed his doctorate in Computer Science at the University of Oxford, where his work in artificial intelligence and natural language processing produced ASKNet, a system for automatically building large-scale semantic networks from text. He went on to serve as a Research and Tutorial Fellow at Keble College, Oxford, and as a Research Scientist in the Medical Informatics Group at the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee.

At UTSC, his research has centred on computer science education and on bringing undergraduates into research. His current work focuses on the implications of conversational AI: how to build agents that are more human, less sycophantic, and that strengthen human connection rather than trying to replace it.
